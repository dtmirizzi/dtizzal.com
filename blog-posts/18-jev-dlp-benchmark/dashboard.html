Binary Detection F1 Score

Is anything sensitive?
Presidio leads at F1=0.735 by being ultra-aggressive — catches 95% of sensitive data but flags 57.5% of clean too. Jev has best precision (0.609) with fewer false alarms.

Precision vs Recall Tradeoff

Detection balance
Presidio optimizes for recall (catch everything). Jev has highest precision — fewer false alarms. Regex is precise but misses 37% of sensitive data.

Per-Category F1 — Who Catches What?

Category coverage
Jev is the only detector covering ALL 5 categories. Presidio & GLiNER are blind to credentials, PHI, and legal. Regex can't detect unstructured data (clean F1=0). Jev's general reasoning adapts without retraining.

False Positive Rate on 87 Clean Samples

Lower is better
All detectors struggle with clean data that "looks" sensitive (fake names in synthetic emails). Jev has the lowest FPR at 49.4% — still high but best at not crying wolf.

False Negative Rate — What Gets Missed

79 sensitive samples
Presidio misses almost nothing (FNR=5.1%). GLiNER & Jev tie at 15.2%. Regex misses 37% — can only catch known patterns. Jev's misses: obfuscated credentials, subtle PHI.

Latency at Scale

Throughput matters
Presidio is 57× faster than Jev (28ms vs 1,570ms). For 1M documents: Presidio ~8 hrs, Jev ~18 days. But Jev catches what speed can't buy — API keys, medical records, legal docs invisible to others.

Category Coverage Radar

Completeness
Jev is the only model with non-zero coverage everywhere. Presidio = PII specialist. GLiNER = PII + some financial. Regex = known patterns only. Jev fills every gap — especially credentials & legal.
